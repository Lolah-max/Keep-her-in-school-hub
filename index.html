    Good to know

    • Change a pad every 4 to 6 hours, or sooner if it is full.
    • Wash hands before and after changing. Wrap used pads and put them in a bin.
    • Periods are normal. No girl should miss school or feel shame because of one.
    • Cycles often last 21 to 35 days and may be irregular at first.
    • Talk to a nurse, doctor or trusted adult if bleeding is very heavy, pain is severe, or periods stop for 3 months or more.
